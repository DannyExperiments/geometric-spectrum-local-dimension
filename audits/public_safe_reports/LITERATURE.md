# Literature comparison

Chitanga, Lemmens and Nussbaum ask in §9 whether the augmented geometric spectrum is nowhere dense between t and the dimension obtained by omitting q, for q≥3. Chitanga, Lemmens and Nussbaum establish the interval below t and nowhere-density results higher up. Chitanga’s thesis extends the latter to upper subregions of the interval in question, and already uses the same geometric tail factor in its breakpoint estimates. Uniform truncation and quantitative first-difference separation on every slice above t give the exact profile in this family. The profile has the form proposed by Das and Simmons.

The primary-source and theorem-architecture search through November 27, 2026 found no exact collision for the claimed scope. Confidence is moderate. Related work on Fibonacci digit alphabets by Dawar and Priyadarshi treats a different family. The companion catalog problem Q1537 counts components for monomial alphabets as the exponent varies.
