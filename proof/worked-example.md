# Worked example

When q=3, t≈0.31546. For a positive anchor a=log(3/2)/log(3) above t, the geometric tail coefficient is 4/5; the correction factor for the first differing digit tends to one as its index increases. At d in the spectrum above t, letting a approach d from below and b approach d from above in the upper and lower arguments gives exponent t/d.
