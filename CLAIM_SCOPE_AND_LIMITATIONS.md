# Claim scope

For each integer q≥2, the dimension spectrum for digits {1,q,q²,…} is nowhere dense above t=log(2)/(2log(q)). At a dimension d in this spectrum the local Hausdorff dimension equals 1 for d≤t and t/d for d>t. For a>t the upper box dimension of the slice at or above a is at most t/a.

The topology above the threshold and the local dimension profile are proved for the augmented geometric alphabets. The general conformal iterated-function-system conjecture remains outside this result. Finite replay checks the symbolic and numerical examples used to illustrate the analytic proof. The manuscript contains the proof. No Lean formalization was attempted. External peer review is pending.
